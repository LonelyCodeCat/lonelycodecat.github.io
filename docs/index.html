How to read the schedule

A regular session runs from 17:00 to 16:00 the next day in Chicago. Monday through Thursday the market pauses for one hour before reopening. Friday’s close starts the weekend break, which ends on Sunday at 17:00 Chicago time.

These two sample weeks show scheduled hours in U.S. daylight and standard time. Holiday schedules, expiring contracts and unscheduled halts can differ.
